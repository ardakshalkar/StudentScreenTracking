STUDENT EXTENSION

Share your workspace

Choose a screen or application window. Your teacher sees only that source. No webcam, microphone, audio, keystrokes, clipboard, or files are accessed.

HTTPS is the default. LAN HTTP exposes screenshots and credentials to network interception; use it only on a trusted classroom network.

Your classroom may require Google sign-in from an approved school domain.

Before you share

Your selected screen/window is transmitted to the classroom server above for your teacher to view. The teacher can enable periodic screenshot recording and optional uploads to their Google Drive. Anything visible in the selected source may be included. Close private content first.

Keep this extension window open while sharing. You can switch to VS Code, Jupyter, PyCharm, or any other application. Closing this window or Chrome’s “Stop sharing” control ends capture. A closed or minimized source window may stop updating; sharing the full screen is more reliable.

Your name and ID are remembered on this device. The teacher may enable periodic screenshot recording; the status above shows the current setting. Stop Sharing ends transmission immediately.
