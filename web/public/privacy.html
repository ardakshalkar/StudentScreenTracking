Classroom Screen Sharing
Privacy & data use

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · extension 1.0.2

This extension connects you to the classroom server your teacher or institution provides. It does not provide a shared public classroom server. Before joining, confirm the server address with your teacher and ask who operates it.

What is collected

When you choose to join, your name, student ID and classroom code are sent to your selected classroom server. If that server requires Google sign-in, Google verifies your account and the server receives your verified email, profile name, account identifier and managed-domain claim. Your verified email becomes the classroom student ID.

Only after you acknowledge the sharing disclosure, click Start Sharing, and select a source in Chrome's permission dialog does the extension capture the chosen screen or application window. Compressed screenshots and capture/connection health messages are transmitted to your classroom server. Screenshots can contain any sensitive information visible in that source. The server also receives ordinary network information such as your IP address and request timing; server logs may retain request metadata.

Purpose and recipients

Data is used to identify students, show their selected screens to the authenticated teacher, and optionally provide periodic screenshot recordings and playback. The classroom server operator and authorized teacher can access these screens and recordings. If the teacher connects Google Drive and enables recording, screenshot ZIP archives may be uploaded to the teacher's Google Drive account. Google processes sign-in and Drive data under its own terms. The extension publisher does not receive a separate copy through analytics or a central collection service built into this product.

What is not accessed

The extension does not request webcam, microphone or audio capture. It does not read keystrokes, clipboard data, browser history or unrelated local files through browser APIs. Browser pages and sensitive content may nevertheless appear inside a screen/window that you explicitly select. There are no advertising or analytics trackers in the extension. Screen sharing never starts automatically.

Local storage and security

Chrome extension storage remembers your server, classroom details, name/ID, and an application-scoped student session credential for reconnection. Those credentials are not Google's access/refresh tokens. Teacher Google credentials stay on the backend and are never given to the student extension. HTTPS/WSS is the default for remote classrooms. An explicit trusted-LAN HTTP option permits private IPv4 server addresses only when both the teacher server and student enable it; screenshots, credentials and passwords are then unencrypted over the network. This option supports code-only joining, with Google sign-in and connecting Drive disabled. HTTP loopback remains available for development. Never enter a server address you do not trust.

Recording and retention

Live sharing and screenshot recording are separate. Recording starts off in a new classroom; the teacher can enable it. The usual recording interval is 20 seconds, configurable from 5 to 3,600 seconds. Changed screenshots are saved as WebP images in ZIP batches, with student IDs and server receipt timestamps. Continuous video is not recorded. The sharing window displays recording status.

The backend's default screenshot retention is 30 days, configurable by the classroom operator. Local screenshots and playback indexes are deleted by the retention worker; uploaded Drive copies are also scheduled for deletion, retrying if Google is unavailable. Classroom/student identity metadata remains in the server database until the operator removes it. Request the applicable retention period and deletion of server-side data from your teacher/institution. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension data but does not delete classroom recordings.

Your controls

You may cancel Chrome's picker, choose a different source, or stop using Stop Sharing in the extension window, toolbar popup, or Chrome's sharing control. Closing the sharing window ends capture. Session end stops connected capture; a network outage longer than the 30-second connection lease also stops it. A short interruption may reconnect only while the originally authorized browser capture session remains active. After a longer interruption you must start sharing again.

Limited use and disclosures

Data is not sold or used for advertising, credit decisions or unrelated profiling by this software. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Classroom operators are responsible for their own access policies, applicable consents, safeguarding student data and responding to requests. The product does not authorize covert monitoring.

Contact

For classroom access, recordings, retention or deletion requests, contact the teacher/institution that supplied your server address. For extension defects or publisher questions, use the publisher's Support contact on its Chrome Web Store listing. Before publication, the publisher must make this policy available at its public privacy-policy URL and confirm its support details.
